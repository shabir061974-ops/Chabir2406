```
PRIVACY POLICY — FAIHA STORE

Last updated: [DATE]

Faiha Co-operative Society ("Faiha", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Faiha Store mobile
application ("the App") and the website www.faihacoopkw.com ("the Site"). This Privacy Policy
explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that
information when you use the App.

By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. WHO WE ARE
Faiha Co-operative Society
[Full registered address, Kuwait]
Contact: [support email] · [phone]

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect only the information needed to provide our shopping and delivery service:

a) Account information (when you create an account):
   - Your name
   - Your mobile phone number (used to sign in)
   - A password (stored only in encrypted/hashed form — we never see your plain password)
   - Your email address (optional)

b) Order and delivery information (when you place an order):
   - Your name and phone number
   - Email address (optional)
   - Delivery address (optional, to deliver your order)
   - The items you order, order totals, any coupon codes, and your order history
   - Your chosen payment method (currently Cash on Delivery)

c) Information we do NOT collect:
   - We do NOT collect your device location (GPS).
   - We do NOT access your contacts, photos, camera, files, or microphone.
   - We do NOT collect payment card or bank details in the App. Orders are paid by Cash on
     Delivery. If online card payment (e.g. KNET) is offered in future, it will be handled by
     the licensed payment provider and this policy will be updated.
   - We do NOT use third-party advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs in the App.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We use your information to:
   - Create and manage your account;
   - Process, deliver, and track your orders;
   - Apply coupons and calculate prices;
   - Contact you about your orders and provide customer support;
   - Keep the App secure and prevent fraud or abuse;
   - Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their
own marketing.

4. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS SHARED
Your information is stored on our own secured systems. We may share limited information only
with:
   - Our delivery staff/partners, solely to deliver your order (name, phone, address);
   - Service providers who host or maintain our systems under confidentiality obligations;
   - Authorities, where required by applicable law in the State of Kuwait.

5. DATA SECURITY
All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS. Passwords are
stored in hashed form. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect
your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect
your data.

6. DATA RETENTION
We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active or as needed
to provide the service and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES
You may:
   - Access and update your account details in the App;
   - Request a copy of your personal data;
   - Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

To request data deletion or exercise any right, contact us at [deletion/support email] or
use [deletion request URL/form]. We will respond within a reasonable period and in line with
applicable law. Note that some records (e.g. completed-order records) may be retained where
the law requires.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
The App is intended for general audiences and for customers of Faiha Co-operative Society. It
is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will
delete it.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date will reflect the
latest version. Material changes will be communicated within the App or on our Site.

10. CONTACT US
Faiha Co-operative Society
[Address, Kuwait]
Email: [support email]
Phone: [phone]
```
