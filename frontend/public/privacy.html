Privacy Policy

Faiha Co-operative Society · Faiha Store (website & mobile app)
Last updated: 15 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Faiha Co-operative Society ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Faiha Store website and mobile applications (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, and (optionally) email address when you create an account.
  • Order information: the products you order, delivery address, order totals, and order history.
  • Login credentials: your password, which is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form and is never visible to us.
  • Technical data: basic device and connection information needed to operate and secure the Service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process, prepare, and deliver your orders.
  • To manage your account and show your order history.
  • To contact you about your orders and provide customer support.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as necessary to fulfil your orders (for example, with delivery staff) or where required by law. We do not share your data with advertisers.

Data Security

All communication with the Service is encrypted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored using strong one-way hashing. Access to customer data is restricted to authorised staff.

Data Retention

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account at any time.

Your Rights

You may access or update your profile from within the app or website at any time. To request a copy of your data or deletion of your account, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
